Kento Momota

"The Comeback King" • Japan

A player defined by extraordinary resilience. In 2020, Momota survived a near-fatal car accident that left him with serious facial injuries. Despite the physical and psychological scars, he returned to professional competition with unbroken determination. On court, his impenetrable defence and delicate net play make him a complete player. His calm demeanour hides a fierce competitive spirit – a testament to his strong mentality.

  • 🏸 World Champion: 2018, 2019
  • 🏸 Record 11 BWF titles in a single season (2019)
  • 🏸 Asian Champion: 2018, 2019

Lin Dan

"Super Dan" • China

Two-time Olympic champion and five-time World champion. Known for his explosive speed, accurate shot quality and strong mentality especially at key points.

  • 🏸 Olympic Gold: 2008, 2012
  • 🏸 World Championships: 5 titles
  • 🏸 All England: 6 titles

Lee Chong Wei

"The Warrior" • Malaysia

Admired for his strong offensive playstyle. He held the world No. 1 ranking for 349 weeks and is regarded as one of the greatest players never to win an Olympic or World title.

  • 🏸 Olympic Silver: 2008, 2012, 2016
  • 🏸 World Championships: 3 silver, 1 bronze
  • 🏸 All England: 4 titles

Shi Yu Qi

"The New Era" • China

Often described as the beginning of a new badminton era, Shi Yu Qi combines speed, power and tactical intelligence. The young Chinese star has already claimed major titles and is seen as the future of men's singles.

  • 🏸 Asian Games Gold: 2022 (held 2023)
  • 🏸 World Tour Finals Champion: 2023
  • 🏸 World No. 1 (career high)
