Lin Dan

“Super Dan” • China

Two-time Olympic champion and five-time World champion. Known for his explosive speed, pinpoint accuracy, and unmatched mental toughness.

  • 🏸 Olympic Gold: 2008, 2012
  • 🏸 World Championships: 5 titles
  • 🏸 All England: 6 titles

Lee Chong Wei

“The Warrior” • Malaysia

A legend of the game, holding the world No. 1 ranking for 349 weeks. Admired for his lightning smashes and never‑say‑die attitude.

  • 🏸 Olympic Silver: 2008, 2012, 2016
  • 🏸 World Championships: 3 silver, 1 bronze
  • 🏸 All England: 4 titles

Tai Tzu‑ying

“The Magician” • Chinese Taipei

Renowned for her deceptive shots and incredible racket skills. She held the world No. 1 spot for a record 214 weeks.

  • 🏸 Olympic Silver: 2020
  • 🏸 World Championships: 1 silver, 2 bronze
  • 🏸 Asian Games: 1 gold

Viktor Axelsen

“The Great Dane” • Denmark

Olympic and World champion known for his towering height and relentless attacking game. Currently one of the most dominant players on tour.

  • 🏸 Olympic Gold: 2020
  • 🏸 World Championships: 2 titles
  • 🏸 All England: 2 titles
